# Competitive Landscape — the integration gap

No major SHM vendor offers all four components (wireless IoT + CV + digital twin + predictive AI) in one open system. **That is our wedge.**

## Vendor matrix

| Tier | Vendors | What they have | What they LACK |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IoT-only** | Resensys (SenSpot), SENSR (CX1), Campbell Scientific, Geokon, Senceive (FlatMesh) | Wireless/wired sensing | CV, twin, ML anomaly |
| **AI-assessment-only** | Sensequake | Consulting + site visits | No always-on monitoring |
| **Twin-only** | Bentley iTwin, Autodesk Tandem, Esri, Willow | BIM/geospatial twins | Live MEMS + ML + CV; credit-metered |
| **Service-heavy** | Strainstall, Encardio/Proqio | Full service | Closed, expensive |

## Verified pricing

- **Bentley iTwin: $199/mo Standard / $499/mo Premium** ✓ (developer.bentley.com/pricing).

## The India angle

- **SPPL India** (IIT Delhi, OSHMAS) — hardware + service heavy, **closed stack**. You are NOT first-mover; your wedge is **open, dataset-driven, low-cost, 4-in-1**.
- Encardio now has **Proqio** (AI) — never claim "no AI". HBM = **HBK** (Monitor360 "Powered by AI"). NI = Emerson T&M (InsightCM is rotating-machinery, not bridge SHM).
