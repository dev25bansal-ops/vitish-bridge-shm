# Mission

> **Prevent the next Morbi.** IoT sensors + computer vision + digital twin + predictive maintenance, fused into one auditable Bridge Health Index — the low-cost tier of the Government's NBHMS architecture.

One line, as approved in [[Home]]: we are the open, low-cost reference implementation of the architecture MoRTH and IIT-M/C-DAC are already mandating.

## Why (verified stats)

- **Morbi, 30 Oct 2022** — pedestrian suspension bridge, ~135 dead (some tallies 141). The inspection regime failed.
- **Gambhira, 9 Jul 2025** — 22 dead. Aftermath: Gujarat inspected 1,800+ bridges → 20 fully closed, 113 partially closed.
- **Global:** 52 (Meizhou), 38 (Shangluo), 13 (Brazil), 12 (Jianzha) in the last two years alone.
- **US systemic:** 623,218 bridges; ~41,600 "poor condition"; 1 in 3 needs repair; ASCE grade C.

## Policy tailwind

- MoRTH IBMS circular (25 Jun 2026): nationwide digital survey of ~1.7 lakh NH bridges, deadline **30 Sep 2026**.
- IIT Madras + C-DAC NBHMS MoU (9 Jul 2026): the national architecture we mirror.

See [[India-Policy]] for details and [[Verified-Facts]] for what must NOT be quoted.

## Success criteria (demo-day)

1. All 4 mandated components demonstrable live through the real pipeline ([[Four-Components]]).
2. BHI drops 87 → 12 on a scripted damage beat, with uncertainty band, on screen.
3. Confusion matrix by Z24 scenario shown, not a single F1 ([[Metrics]]).
4. 6-minute storyboard delivered with every source labeled ([[Storyboard]]).
5. Team answers any judge question from the [[QandA-Prep]] bank without overclaiming.
