Key-Decisions #5

The sun moves the bridge more than the damage does.

Z24's modal frequencies swing roughly 10 percent with seasonal temperature, versus 1 to 2 percent for actual structural damage. Without temperature compensation, the system would flag weather as damage. With it, only real damage makes it through.

Cited: Neumann et al., arXiv:2409.17735. Ridge regression temperature compensation baseline trained on 1997-1998 continuous Swiss environmental cycle.

FAQ

The questions the jury asks.

Direct, unhedged answers to real engineering and deployment questions.

Would VITISH have caught Morbi?
No. Morbi was a 140-year-old steel suspension footbridge carrying 500 people against a rating of 125, with rusted cables, broken anchors, and loose bolts. It had no sensors. It is structurally the opposite of the Z24. The global-vibration monitoring VITISH performs today has a known blind spot for cable-anchorage corrosion and overload failure modes. The roadmap adds strain-gauge and acoustic-emission sensing to close that blind spot. Morbi is our motivation. Z24 is our proof.
How is this different from Sydney Harbour's 2,400 sensors?
Sydney Harbour is the gold standard, and it costs gold-standard money. VITISH is the reference implementation of the architecture India can actually afford to deploy across 1.7 lakh NH bridges. The low-cost tier beneath Sydney Harbour's 2,400 sensors.
Is the demo real or simulated?
The hero bridge carries the real Z24 progressive-damage campaign end to end. The digital twin's measured first-mode frequency slides 3.80 to 3.23 Hz exactly as the seeded tendon-rupture predicts. The 49 other bridges are illustrative, real locations, simulated health. Every data source is labelled on screen so the audience never confuses what is measured from what is modeled.
What about the ML model's real-world performance?
The demo healthy-versus-rupture P=R=F1=1.0. The real Z24 P=0.5263, R=0.0016, F1=0.0032. The published VAE+OCSVM numbers from Scientific Reports 2025 are PR 0.996 and recall 0.999. We cite all three honestly, on screen, in the ProvenancePanel. The spectral-heuristic floor in backend/app/anomaly.py carries the demo when the trained ML ensemble is uninformative.
